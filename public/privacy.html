Privacy

Last updated 10 October 2026

What this site collects

This website does not set cookies, does not run analytics and does not load anything from third-party services. Its fonts, pictures and code are served from the site itself.

The forms

When you use the Contact form, the name, email address and message you enter are delivered to IONA so that we can reply to you. A Hospitality Enquiry also carries your city, your business name and your requirements, for the same purpose. Both are kept for as long as it takes to handle your enquiry and any follow-up.

When you join the launch list, your name, email address and city are kept so that we can send you updates on IONA's launch and availability. You can ask to be removed from the list at any time.

None of this is sold, or shared with anyone else for their marketing.

Settings kept in your browser

The Sound control stores your choice in your browser's local storage so that it is remembered on your next visit. That value never leaves your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Hosting

Like every website, the servers that deliver this site keep standard technical logs, such as the IP address and browser type of each request, for security and to keep the service running. They are not used to identify you.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete anything you sent through the forms. Write to us through the Contact form on the home page and we will respond.

Changes

If the way this site handles information changes, this page will be updated and the date above will move.
